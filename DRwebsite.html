A big project that I’m working on currently is a neural network designed to detect and identify text that was generated by large language models (LLMs). I plan to use the six LLMs ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Muse Spark, Grok, and DeepSeek. I could have used more LLMs, but I did research into the specific way I want to build the neural network and I found that it would be less accurate if I gave it more things to distinguish between. I’m not sure why I decided on six LLMs specifically and not more or less, but if it works out, it works out.
