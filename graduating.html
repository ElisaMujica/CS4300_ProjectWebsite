So, You Graduated... Now What?

A little guide to life after college.

Congratulations, you made it! Whether you have a job lined up or are still figuring things out, remember that graduation is not the finish line. It's the beginning of a new chapter.

Already Have a Job? Lucky...

Starting your career is exciting, but remember that your job isn't your entire life. Here are a few things to keep in mind:

1. Take Care of Yourself

It's not always about work. Don't give up sleep, exercise, hobbies, and rest just to make an impact. Your health is just as important as your career.

2. Maintain Social Relationships

Remember to stay connected with friends, family, and the those who supported you along the way.

3. Try Something New

Pick up a hobby, travel, volunteer, or learn a new skill.

4. Keep Learning

Continue developing your skills, exploring opportunities, and learning from your experiences.

Still Looking for a Job? Tell me about!

Don't worry. Not everyone has it figured out. Finding the right opportunity takes time.

1. Update Your Resume and Portfolio

Highlight your education, projects, internships, volunteer experience, and technical skills. Remember that experience comes in many forms.

2. Use Professional Networking

Connect with classmates, professors, former coworkers, and professionals in your field.

3. Explore Job Search Resources

Places to start your search:

  • Gator Success Institute - Best place to start when you don't know where.
  • LinkedIn Jobs - Find job openings and connect with professionals.
  • CareerOneStop - Explore career information, job search advice, and employment resources.
