About me

Hi, I'm Edwin Olvera Quintos. I'm a junior at the University of Houston-Downtown working toward a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, and I expect to graduate in May 2027. I'm based in Houston and speak both English and Spanish fluently.

My computer science coursework has included parallel computing and bioinformatics, and I'm especially interested in artificial intelligence and robotics. Python is the language I keep building my skills in, both for my classes and on my own time.

I also have customer-facing experience from working as a sales advisor and customer service representative at Best Buy in Houston. That job had me helping customers every day, often explaining technology in plain, simple language so they could make the right choice for their needs.

Outside of school, I like to read a lot, and I enjoy spending time outdoors whenever I get the chance. Being outdoors is a welcome break from the hours I spend in front of a screen for school. I also collect comic books, especially Invincible. Between a good book, a new comic, and some time outside, I always have something to look forward to after a long week of classes and coding.
